## Full description (max 4000 chars)
PocketWise (省省账) is a clean, bilingual personal finance tracker built for people who want to actually understand where their money goes — without spreadsheets or clutter.

TRACK EVERYTHING IN ONE PLACE
Log expenses and income across cash, bank accounts, credit cards, and e-wallets. See your net worth and per-account balances update instantly.

BUDGETS THAT ACTUALLY HELP
Set spending limits by category and get warned before you go over — not after.

QUICK ADD WITH AI
Type or say what you spent in plain language ("lunch RM15 at the mamak") and PocketWise fills in the category, merchant, and account for you.

RECURRING & UPCOMING PAYMENTS
Set up recurring bills and subscriptions once, and get reminded before they're due.

SAVINGS PROJECTS
Save toward a goal — a wedding, a renovation, a trip — and track progress separately from everyday spending.

REPORTS YOU CAN ACTUALLY READ
Daily, weekly, and monthly breakdowns with charts, plus one-tap export to Excel or PDF.

BUILT FOR PRIVACY AND SPEED
Lock the app with a PIN, sign in securely with your Google account, and keep everything synced across devices. No ads, ever.

BILINGUAL BY DESIGN
Switch instantly between English and Chinese (中文) — the whole app, not just a few labels.

MULTI-CURRENCY
Track accounts in different currencies with automatic formatting.

Whether you're budgeting month to month or saving for something big, PocketWise keeps your finances organized, honest, and easy to check at a glance.
